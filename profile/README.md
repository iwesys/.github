# IWE — Intellectual Work Environment

**An IDE for thinking.** Just as an IDE unifies editor, compiler, and debugger for a programmer, IWE unifies knowledge, planning, and AI agents into one environment for intellectual work.

## You are the pilot, not the project

Stop treating yourself as the thing to optimize — build the system in which you are the pilot. The superpowers aren't in the armor; they're in the person inside it. IWE turns you and your AI agents into a **pilot + suit ensemble**: an exoskeleton that amplifies your thinking and never replaces it. After every session you can do more — and explain it without *"the AI told me."*

## Everyone has the all-knowing AI. Yours should actually know *you*.

A useful AI is assembled from **six types of information — and four of them exist only in your life.** No vendor can collect them for you. That's why IWE is a personal environment you grow, not another chat window you open.

<details>
<summary><b>How it works — the six types</b></summary>

| # | Type of information | Where it comes from |
|---|--------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Model knowledge** — everything the LLM already knows | The AI vendor |
| 2 | **First principles** — quality filters for reasoning and decisions | Open FPF corpus by [Anatoly Levenchuk](https://github.com/ailev/FPF); IWE plugs it in |
| 3 | **Stable facts about you** — your stage, goals, constraints | Your life; written once, partly accumulated by the AI |
| 4 | **Your activity stream** — what you actually do and finish | Your daily work; best collected automatically |
| 5 | **Your formalized knowledge base** — your personal Pack: your domain, your distinctions, your rules | Only you can build it |
| 6 | **Your decision chronicle** — the working practice that can't be formalized | Only you, through practice itself |

The vendor sells you rows 1–2. Rows 3–6 can't be bought — they accumulate in your environment as you work. That accumulated structure is what makes *your* AI useful, and it compounds every session.

</details>

## A guide that rewrites itself around your work

This is where the six types converge: a personal guide compiled from your diagnosed stage, your real activity, and your own knowledge base — and recompiled as you move, around the work you actually did. Not a one-size-fits-all course. The same guide engine runs standalone as `guide-kit`, powers the hosted platform, and ships inside the template as it hardens — one engine, not three.

## One session, two outcomes

This is not a course platform. The same session that **ships your result** also **updates your knowledge base** — nothing to "study later." The work is the curriculum.
